# Data, models and dependency attribution

- **QASPER v0.3** is by Pradeep Dasigi and colleagues at Ai2. Cite their 2021 paper, *A Dataset of Information-Seeking Questions and Answers Anchored in Research Papers*. The dataset card lists CC BY 4.0. Underlying papers can carry individual rights, so the proposed release excludes full texts, excerpts, questions, reference answers, raw annotations, and archives. Download the official archives using the setup and reproduction instructions. [Paper](https://aclanthology.org/2021.naacl-main.365/) · [Dataset card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/allenai/qasper/blob/9834d7e5a315da3a8c5df41024929af4462b80ce/README.md) · [arXiv licenses](https://info.arxiv.org/help/license/index.html)
- **BGE-small-en-v1.5** is published by BAAI under MIT. **ms-marco-MiniLM-L6-v2** is published by Sentence Transformers under Apache-2.0. The setup uses hash-pinned official ONNX exports. No weights are bundled. [BGE card](https://huggingface.co/BAAI/bge-small-en-v1.5) · [MiniLM card](https://huggingface.co/cross-encoder/ms-marco-MiniLM-L6-v2)
- **Retrieval dependencies** are NumPy (BSD-3-Clause), ONNX Runtime (MIT), Hugging Face Tokenizers (Apache-2.0), rank-bm25 (Apache-2.0), and psutil (BSD-3-Clause). They are installed separately from their package distributions. [NumPy](https://numpy.org/doc/stable/license.html) · [ONNX Runtime](https://github.com/microsoft/onnxruntime/blob/main/LICENSE) · [Tokenizers](https://github.com/huggingface/tokenizers/blob/main/LICENSE) · [rank-bm25](https://github.com/dorianbrown/rank_bm25/blob/master/LICENSE) · [psutil](https://github.com/giampaolo/psutil/blob/master/LICENSE)
- **Display and plotting** use Streamlit (Apache-2.0), Altair (BSD-3-Clause), and optional Matplotlib (see its PSF-based license). Their distributions include dependency notices. [Streamlit](https://github.com/streamlit/streamlit/blob/develop/LICENSE) · [Altair](https://github.com/vega/altair/blob/main/LICENSE) · [Matplotlib](https://matplotlib.org/stable/project/license.html)
- **Archived generator feasibility** used official Apache-2.0 Qwen2.5 checkpoints and MIT-licensed llama.cpp and llama-cpp-python. Those runtimes and weights are outside the local retrieval setup and are excluded from publication. [1.5B](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct-GGUF) · [7B](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct-GGUF) · [llama.cpp](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/blob/master/LICENSE) · [llama-cpp-python](https://github.com/abetlen/llama-cpp-python/blob/main/LICENSE.md)
